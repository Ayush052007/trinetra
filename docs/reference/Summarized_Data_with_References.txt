SECTION 1: THE MAIN PROBLEM
================================================================================

PROBLEM: Police have massive data but cannot connect the dots.

• 80% of law enforcement agencies struggle to analyze their own data.
  Source: Cognyte / Kaseware, 2025
  Link: https://www.precedenceresearch.com/agentic-ai-in-law-enforcement-and-surveillance-market

• Police departments hold millions of records in separate silos.
  "Every police department is swimming in data... most of this information lives
   in separate silos, making it incredibly tough for investigators to connect the
   dots, and critical connections get lost in the shuffle."
  Source: ARMS Public Safety / Industry Analysis

• Manual network analysis is difficult, time-consuming, and error-prone.
  "Manual examination of social networks tends to be difficult, time consuming,
   and arbitrary, making it more prone to error."
  Source: Xu & Chen, ACM Communications, 2005
  Link: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1064830.1064835

SECTION 9: KEY TAKEAWAYS
================================================================================

1. CRIMINALS ARE CONNECTED
   85% of gun violence happens inside one social network. Criminal groups span
   50+ countries. These connections are invisible without graph analysis.

2. DATA EXISTS BUT IS UNUSABLE
   80% of agencies cannot analyze their own data. 7+ million records in one
   city alone. Unstructured narratives remain "largely untapped."

3. CASES ARE GOING COLD
   352,390 unsolved US homicides. Clearance rates dropped from 90% to 61%.
   6,280 unsolved in 2024 alone.

4. THE MONEY IS MASSIVE
   $4 trillion global illicit economy. $88–114 billion SE Asia scams.
   $20.9 billion US cybercrime losses (2025).

5. AI CAN HELP — BUT ADOPTION IS LOW
   92% accuracy in leader identification. 4.3x faster than manual.
   70% useful paths vs. 30% manual. Market growing at 38.40% CAGR.
